**DeathList** remembers the players who killed you and warns you when one of them shows up again.

When a player kills you, a window asks if you want to put them on your list. You can write down why.

When someone from your list is near, you get:

- a skull over their nameplate
- a warning in the middle of the screen
- a sound
- a chat line with your note

### Commands

- `/dl` opens the options
- `/dl add` adds your target, or the player who killed you last
- `/dl add Name, note` adds a player by name
- `/dl remove Name`
- `/dl list`

In the options you can edit notes, remove players, and export or import your list.
